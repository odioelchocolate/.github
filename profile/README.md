# Odio el Chocolate 🍫

A strange school, a chocolate hunt, and four ways to play. **Odio el Chocolate** is a Baldi’s Basics mod by Wantho, and [odioelchocolate.net](https://odioelchocolate.net/) is an independent place to discover it.

**[Play Odio el Chocolate in your browser →](https://odioelchocolate.net/#play)**

New to the game? Start with Low Cortisol Mode, keep an eye on the chocolate counter and stamina meter, then explore Main, Baltazar, and Triple Trouble modes. Our site brings together a [player guide](https://odioelchocolate.net/guide), authentic screenshots, gameplay videos, and quick answers to help you get started.

Prefer the creator’s downloadable releases? Visit [Wantho’s original Windows page](https://wantho.itch.io/odio-el-chocolate) or the [Android release](https://wantho.itch.io/android-odio-el-chocolate).
